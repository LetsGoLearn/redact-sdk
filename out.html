STUDENT SG demonstrates a continued need for specially designed instruction due to social-emotional and behavioral regulation deficits that adversely

impact

her ability to access and participate in the general education curriculum. While she exhibits age-appropriate academic skills, she experiences difficulty

managing frustration, responding to perceived failure, and adapting to changes in routine. These challenges may result in behavioral meltdowns, task

avoidance, and decreased engagement in classroom activities. As a result, STUDENT SG requires explicit instruction in emotional regulation, coping

strategies, and self-management, as well as structured supports and opportunities for guided practice across settings.

Without these supports, STUDENT SG is likely to have difficulty maintaining consistent participation, completing tasks, and benefiting from instruction in

the general education environment.
